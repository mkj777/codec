## Corrections made during implementation

- **Avalonia has no `TextShadow`.** The cinema text shadows became `DropShadowEffect` on `Visual.Effect`.
- **`Button` has no `BoxShadow`.** The Play pill's orange bloom is a `DropShadowEffect` instead; the
  reference's `-10px` spread is not expressible and was dropped.
- **`ScanningView` was unreachable.** It was written per the spec, then removed: onboarding's closing
  step is where the first run actually lands, so the scanning screen was folded into that step,
  bound to `ScanScreenTitle` and driven by `OnboardingView.FindGames_Click`.
- **`saturate()` and `contrast()` have no Avalonia equivalent.** Cinema's `saturate(1.06)
  contrast(1.03)` was dropped as visually negligible.
- **Base `TextBlock` no longer sets `Foreground`.** It was overriding button content colour
  everywhere; letting it inherit lets a button colour its own label.

## Known gaps

- The empty-library state, the notice pill, and the onboarding flow were not exercised at runtime.
  They are reachable only from an empty library or an active scan, neither of which existed on the
  test machine.
- The sidebar scrollbar renders at 8 px rather than the reference's 5 px. Narrowing it requires
  crossing two nested template boundaries; the single global width was kept instead.
